# Practical diffusion models

Lecturer and seminarian: [Nikita Starodubcev](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=o6pRm_gAAAAJ&hl=ru)

## Annotation

This week, we explore the practical aspects of diffusion models. The lecture covers conditional generation with classifier-free guidance, training objectives and noise schedules, and the differences between pixel-space and latent diffusion. We also discuss image quality evaluation, modern architectures such as U-Net and Diffusion Transformers (DiT), and popular models including Stable Diffusion and FLUX. We then examine numerical samplers, including Euler and Heun, and the trade-off between sampling speed and generation quality. In the hands-on seminar, we put these ideas into practice. We implement diffusion models, experiment with different training schedules and sampling algorithms, and compare their performance on state-of-the-art models.
