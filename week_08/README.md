## Annotation

This week, we will begin a series of lectures on generative models. We will start by formalizing the problem of generative modeling and suggesting a general approach to solving it. The main part of the lectures will focus on two classic and fundamental approaches: the variational autoencoder (VAE) and the generative adversarial network (GAN). We will derive these models, discuss their theoretical grounding and their qualitative properties in practice.
