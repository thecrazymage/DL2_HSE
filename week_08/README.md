## Annotation

This week, we will begin a series of lectures on generative models. We will start by formalizing the problem of generative modeling and suggesting a general approach to solving it. The main part of the lectures will focus on one of the classic and fundamental approaches: the variational autoencoder (VAE). Starting from the objective of minimizing discrepancies between generated and observed samples, we will derive ordinary autoencoders and explain why they are not suitable for generation. We will then derive VAEs to address these limitations, establish their connection to autoencoders, discuss their practical properties and place them in the so-called Generative Learning Trilemma.
