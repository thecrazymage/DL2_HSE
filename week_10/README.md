## Annotation

In this lecture, we’ll finish our theoretical discussion of generative learning with diffusion models. Last time, we introduced forward processes that gradually add noise to data. This time, we’ll see how the reverse process of gradual denoising is learned in practice and connect this procedure to variational autoencoders. We’ll then introduce the score function, explore its connection to the denoiser learned by a diffusion model, and see how it can be used to adapt sampling for conditional generation.
