## Annotation

In the next lecture, we will continue exploring generative models. We will start with generative adversarial networks (GANs), where a discriminator learns to distinguish real samples from generated ones, while a generator learns to fool it. We will derive their training objective, discuss their theoretical guarantees and behavior in practice.

We will then turn to diffusion models. We'll start by introducing the forward process that gradually adds noise to data, begin exploring how to construct its time reversal, and discuss the connection between diffusion models and variational autoencoders.
