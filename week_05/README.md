# Practical Challenges in Agentic Reinforcement Learning for LLMs

Lecturer and seminarian: [Timofey Smirnov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timothyxp/)

Recordings (in Russian): [lecture and seminar](https://disk.yandex.ru/i/ZXpcR7oBsBIgNg).

## Annotation

This talk covers the practical aspects of agentic RL, focusing on building robust RL harnesses, overcoming obstacles in making agents practically useful, and mitigating instability. We will examine why RL often fails in agentic workflows and explore techniques for constructing stable training signals.
